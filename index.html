Pet Show: Look and Write!

Year 1 English Super Minds 1 · Unit 3

Look at each animal and write one correct sentence.

How to play

  1. Look at the animal picture.
  2. Write I like [animal]. or I don't like [animal].
  3. Start with a capital letter and end with a full stop.
  4. Complete all 8 animals, then check your score.
  5. Take a screenshot of your final score and submit it to Google Classroom.
Example:
Correct: I like spiders.
Incorrect: I like spiders (missing full stop).

You can choose whether you like or don't like each animal. The quiz checks grammar, spelling, capital letters and punctuation.
